Privacy notice

Last updated: 16 October 2026

In short: we keep only what we need to run your money book and answer you. We don’t keep your phone number (except encrypted, for at most a day, to send you one reminder), never sell your data, and you can erase everything by typing DELETE in the chat.

1. Who we are

Sọ̀rọ̀ is a service of Charavol Technologies Ltd, a company registered in Nigeria (the “data controller”). Contact for anything about your data: privacy@charavol.com.

3. Legal basis

We process your data on the basis of your consent, which you give by tapping “I agree” before Sọ̀rọ̀ does anything else (Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, section 25). You can withdraw consent at any time by typing DELETE.

4. Who processes your data for us

We use these service providers, each under contract and only to run Sọ̀rọ̀:

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform): delivers messages between you and Sọ̀rọ̀. WhatsApp’s own privacy policy also applies to your use of WhatsApp.
  • Supabase: our database, hosted in the European Union.
  • Render: runs the server that receives WhatsApp messages, in the European Union (Frankfurt).
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS): runs our AI models (speech recognition, the N-ATLaS language model and voice) in the European Union (Frankfurt).
  • Modal: runs the same AI models on servers in the United States, only when AWS is unavailable, including the few minutes AWS takes to start up after a quiet spell.

The AI models run on our own accounts with these providers; your messages are not used to train anyone else’s AI. Some of these providers are outside Nigeria. We rely on your consent and on the providers’ contractual data-protection safeguards for these transfers (NDPA 2023, sections 41–43).

5. Research and anonymised statistics

We may share anonymised usage records (no phone numbers or hashes, no names) and overall statistics with research and funding partners to show how the service is used. We may also use anonymised transcripts to improve Sọ̀rọ̀’s understanding of the languages it supports.

6. How long we keep it

Until you type DELETE, or 12 months after your last message, whichever comes first. After that, your records are erased. Anonymised statistics may be kept.

7. Your rights

Under the NDPA 2023 you can ask to access, correct, erase or receive a copy of your data, object to or restrict its use, and withdraw consent. Type DELETE in the chat for instant erasure, or email privacy@charavol.com for anything else; we reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng).

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, the database is only reachable with our server’s secret key, phone numbers are hashed before storage (a number kept for a reminder is encrypted with a key the database never holds), and access is limited to the Sọ̀rọ̀ team.

9. Age

Sọ̀rọ̀ is for people aged 18 and over.

10. Changes

If we change this notice in a way that affects you, we’ll tell you in the chat before the change applies.
