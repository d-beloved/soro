Start in three steps

Talk while you work: at your stall, on the road, or between lectures.

1

Say hello on WhatsApp

Tap “Start on WhatsApp”, agree to the privacy terms and pick your language.

2

Send a voice note

“I sell 2 crates of egg, 9k.” “I buy data 1,500.” “Emeka owe me 4k.”

SoroEngine writes it down and shows you what it heard. Wrong amount? Say “no be 5k, na 50k”, or type UNDO.

3

Ask about your money

“Where my money dey go this month?” “How I go save when market slow?”

You get your totals, plus a short money tip as text and as a voice note.

What SoroEngine keeps for you

  • Your business: sales, work you got paid for, shop costs, stock counts, and who owes who.
  • Your own money: what you spend on food, transport, data and rent, money that comes in, and money you lend friends.
  • Budgets: set a monthly limit for food, data or transport, and SoroEngine tells you what’s left each time you spend.
  • Summaries for today, this week or this month, with your biggest spending and who still owes you.
  • Money tips on cash flow, pricing, saving, collecting debts, getting loan-ready, and keeping business and personal money apart. We write each tip ourselves; the AI only rephrases it.
  • Loan requests: tell SoroEngine how much you need and why. Your records show a lender what your business can carry.

Made with Nigerian AI

SoroEngine listens with NCAIR’s speech models for Nigerian-accented English, Yorùbá, Hausa and Igbo, and thinks with N-ATLaS, Nigeria’s own multilingual language model. YarnGPT reads the replies out in Nigerian voices.

N-ATLaS is an initiative of the Federal Ministry of Communications, Innovation and Digital Economy, and powered by Awarri Technologies.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. SoroEngine is free during the pilot. You only use your normal WhatsApp data.

Is it only for traders?

No. Traders, artisans and other small businesses can record sales and costs. Workers and students can track spending and budgets. You can do both in one chat.

Can I speak Pidgin or mix languages?

Yes. Choose English/Pidgin for Pidgin. In Yorùbá, Hausa or Igbo, mixing in English words is fine.

Is it a bank? Does it give loans?

No. SoroEngine is a record book and money coach. It doesn’t hold money or give loans. If you ask for a loan, we keep the request so we can connect you to partner lenders later, and only if you agree.

What if it hears me wrong?

Every reply starts with what SoroEngine heard. To fix an amount, say “no be 5k, na 50k”. To remove the last record, type UNDO, then send it again.
