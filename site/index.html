What SoroEngine does for you

  • Shop book: sales, expenses, stock, and who owes who.
  • Daily, weekly and monthly summaries on request.
  • Money tips on keeping shop money apart, cash flow, pricing, saving, collecting debts and getting loan-ready, all checked by people before you hear them.
  • Loan-ready records: tell us how much you need and why; your records show lenders what your business can carry.

Made with Nigerian AI

SoroEngine listens with NCAIR’s speech models for Nigerian-accented English, Yorùbá, Hausa and Igbo, and thinks with N-ATLaS, Nigeria’s own multilingual language model. Replies are voiced by YarnGPT, which speaks with Nigerian voices.

N-ATLaS is an initiative of the Federal Ministry of Communications, Innovation and Digital Economy, and powered by Awarri Technologies.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. SoroEngine is free during the pilot. You only use your normal WhatsApp data.

Can I speak Pidgin or mix languages?

Yes. Choose English/Pidgin for Pidgin. In Yorùbá, Hausa or Igbo, mixing in English words is fine.

Is it a bank? Does it give loans?

No. SoroEngine is a record book and money coach. It does not hold money or give loans. Loan requests are saved so we can connect you to partner lenders later, only if you agree.

What if it hears me wrong?

Every reply starts with what SoroEngine heard. If it’s wrong, just send the voice note again or type it.
