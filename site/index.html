Start in three steps

Talk while you do your day: on the bus, at your stall, or between lectures.

1

Say hello on WhatsApp

Tap “Start on WhatsApp”, agree to the privacy terms and pick your language.

2

Talk about your money

“I buy data 1,500.” “My salary don enter, 150k.” “I sell 2 crates of egg, 9k.”

Sọ̀rọ̀ writes it down and shows you what it heard. Wrong amount? Say “no be 5k, na 50k”, or type UNDO.

3

Ask where it went

“Where my money dey go this month?” “How I go save small small?”

You get your totals and a short money tip. Ask by voice note and Sọ̀rọ̀ answers with a short voice note too.

What Sọ̀rọ̀ keeps for you

  • Your own money: what you spend on food, transport, data and rent, the money that comes in, and money you lend friends.
  • Budgets: set a monthly limit for food, data or transport, and Sọ̀rọ̀ tells you what’s left each time you spend.
  • Summaries for today, this week or this month, with your biggest spending and who still owes you.
  • Your business, if you have one: sales, paid work, shop costs, stock counts, and who owes who. Business and personal money stay in separate totals.
  • Money tips on saving, budgeting, collecting debts, pricing and getting loan-ready. We write each tip ourselves; the AI only rephrases it.
  • Loan requests: tell Sọ̀rọ̀ how much you need and why. Your records show a lender what you can carry.

Made with Nigerian AI

Sọ̀rọ̀ means “talk” in Yorùbá. It listens with NCAIR’s speech models for Nigerian-accented English, Yorùbá, Hausa and Igbo, and thinks with N-ATLaS, Nigeria’s own multilingual language model. YarnGPT reads short replies out in Nigerian voices.

N-ATLaS is an initiative of the Federal Ministry of Communications, Innovation and Digital Economy, and powered by Awarri Technologies.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Sọ̀rọ̀ is free during the pilot. You only use your normal WhatsApp data.

Is it for my own money or for business?

Both, in one chat. Track your spending, salary, allowance and budgets. If you sell or do paid work, record sales, costs and who owes you, and Sọ̀rọ̀ keeps those totals apart.

Can I speak Pidgin or mix languages?

Yes. Choose English/Pidgin for Pidgin. In Yorùbá, Hausa or Igbo, mixing in English words is fine.

Is it a bank? Does it give loans?

No. Sọ̀rọ̀ is a record book and money coach. It doesn’t hold money or give loans. If you ask for a loan, we keep the request so we can connect you to partner lenders later, and only if you agree.

What if it hears me wrong?

Every reply to a voice note starts with what Sọ̀rọ̀ heard. To fix an amount, say “no be 5k, na 50k”. To remove the last record, type UNDO, then send it again.
