Made with Nigerian AI

Sọ̀rọ̀ means “talk” in Yorùbá. Every step runs on models built for Nigerian voices.

  1. Listens

    NCAIR speech models

    Voice note to text, in Nigerian accents.

  2. Understands

    N-ATLaS

    Works out if it’s a sale, a debt, a budget or a question.

  3. Speaks

    OmniVoice

    Answers back out loud, in your language.

N-ATLaS is an initiative of the Federal Ministry of Communications, Innovation and Digital Economy, and powered by Awarri Technologies.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Just your normal WhatsApp data.

Is it for my own money or for business?

Both, in one chat. Business and personal totals stay apart.

Can I speak Pidgin or mix languages?

Yes. Pick English/Pidgin for Pidgin. Mixing English into Yorùbá, Hausa or Igbo is fine.

Is it a bank? Does it give loans?

No. Sọ̀rọ̀ doesn’t hold money or lend. We have no lending partners yet. If you ask for a loan, we save the request and share it only with your OK once partners join.

What if it hears me wrong?

Every reply shows what Sọ̀rọ̀ heard. Say “no be 5k, na 50k” to fix it, or type UNDO.

Can someone with my phone see my records?

Only if they can open your WhatsApp. Turn on Chat lock for the Sọ̀rọ̀ chat to stop that.
